# Scientific Capability

Record the scientific question, authoritative basis, supported scope, inputs, population,
missingness, outputs, completeness, limitations, provenance, replay proof, and applicability.
Keep scientific meaning in `science/` and safe exposure in `capabilities/`. Never invent a
method to unblock orchestration or hide scope, cohort, threshold, or interpretation changes.
